## Verbal script for the start of a session (not part of the form)

"Thanks for coming. This takes about 25 minutes. First you'll follow a dot around the screen
for half a minute so the software can adjust to your camera. After that you'll look at two
words on the screen and choose the one I name, just by looking at it for about half a second. There's nothing to learn
and nothing you can get wrong — we're testing the camera and the screen design, not you.

The camera is on, but nothing is recorded: no video, no pictures, no sound. The computer only
saves which box you looked at and how long it took.

You can stop any time, and you don't need a reason. If your eyes get tired, say so and we'll
take a break or stop. Any questions before we start?"
